Talking to McGaw Lists

Works on any Echo in the house, the Echo Show and the Fire TV (hold the Alexa button on the remote). On a screen, Alexa shows what she’s talking about — tap or press select on an item to tick it off.

“Alexa, ask family lists…”then say what you want. Or say “Alexa, open family lists” for a summary, then talk without repeating the first bit.

Shopping & adding things

  • add milk and breadGoes on the shared shopping list, in the right aisle
  • add two pints of milkQuantities work: “500 grams of mince”, “a dozen eggs”
  • we’re out of washing up liquid
  • add eggs, bacon and breadSay “and” between things — Alexa doesn’t hear commas
  • add call the dentist to my to do list
  • add chase invoice to my work list
  • put sun cream on the packing list

Things bought before come back off “Used before” — no duplicates.

Reading a list

  • what’s on the shopping list
  • what do we need
  • read my to do list
  • show the work listAny list by name: “show Ongoing Work To-Do List”
  • what’s on the packing list

Ticking things off

  • tick off milk
  • we’ve got eggs and bread
  • cross off book the MOT
  • remove bananas from the shopping list
  • I’ve done the expenses

On the Echo Show or TV: tap (or select) the circle next to an item.

Dinner & the meal plan

  • what’s for dinner / what’s for tea
  • what’s for dinner tomorrowOr any day: “on Friday”
  • who’s cooking tonight
  • what are we having this weekShows the week on screen
  • we’re having lasagne for dinner on Friday

Who’s home for dinner

  • I’ll be late for dinner tonight
  • I’m home for dinner tomorrow

Needs your voice linked (see below) so Alexa knows who “I” is.

Linking your voice

  • 1. In the Alexa app: set up Voice ID for yourself and turn on Personalise skills.
  • 2. In McGaw Lists: Family → Alexa → Link my voice for a 4-digit code.
  • 3. Say Alexa, ask family lists, my code is 1234
  • who am IChecks Alexa knows your voice

Until you’re linked, Alexa only uses lists the whole family shares.

Good to know

  • “Help”Say “help” while the skill is open to hear examples.
  • “Stop” or “cancel”Ends it. The Echo Show screen stays up for a few minutes, then goes back to normal.
  • Never by voiceGift lists and the budget can’t be read or changed through Alexa — no spoilt surprises.
  • She misheard?Speak the list name clearly (“the shopping list”, “my work list”). Anything added by Alexa shows “via Alexa” in the app, so it’s easy to fix.
  • Fire TVHold the Alexa button on the remote and speak. Use the arrows and select to move around and tick things off.
