McGaw Lists — privacy

McGaw Lists is a private app used only by the McGaw family. It isn't available to the public.

What it stores

The family's lists, tasks, meal plans, notes, countdowns and (for two people) a household budget, stored in the family's own Supabase database in London. Only the four family members can sign in.

The Alexa skill

When someone speaks to the skill, Amazon sends the words it recognised to the app so it can add to or read a list. If a person has linked their Alexa voice ID, Amazon sends an anonymous voice identifier, which the app stores alongside that family member's name so it knows whose lists to use. No recordings are stored by the app. Anyone can unlink their voice from the app (Family → Alexa → Forget) or by turning off “Personalise skills” in the Alexa app.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone outside the family.
